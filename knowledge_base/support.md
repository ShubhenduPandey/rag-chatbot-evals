# Atlas Workspace — fictional support policies

## Subscription cancellation

An owner can cancel from Settings, Billing. Cancellation stops the next renewal but does not immediately remove access. The workspace keeps its paid features until the end of the current billing period. Cancellation does not delete projects. Export any required data before separately deleting a workspace.

## Refund policy

Atlas's illustrative refund policy allows a full refund within 14 days of the first payment. Renewal payments are not refundable. The owner must contact support with the workspace ID and payment date. Refund requests do not automatically cancel the subscription; cancellation is a separate action in Billing.

## Support hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC, excluding public holidays. The target first response is one business day, not a guaranteed resolution time. There is no weekend phone support. Urgent reports can be emailed at any time and are reviewed during the next support window.

## Data location and deletion

This fictional product handbook specifies an EU data region. Owners can request a workspace data export before deletion. Workspace deletion is permanent. Project deletion has a separate 30-day recovery period. This demo does not store real Atlas customer data and does not establish any actual compliance certification.
