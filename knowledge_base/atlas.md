# Atlas Workspace — fictional product handbook

## Getting started

Atlas is a fictional team workspace used only for this RAG demonstration. Create a workspace, invite your team, and add a project. A project groups tasks, documents, and milestones. This handbook describes an illustrative product, not a real service or commercial offer.

## Roles and permissions

Atlas has three roles: owner, editor, and viewer. Owners manage billing, membership, and workspace deletion. Editors create and update projects and tasks but cannot change billing or delete a workspace. Viewers can read projects and leave comments but cannot change tasks. Only an owner can change a member's role.

## Inviting guests

An owner can invite a guest to one selected project. Guests cannot browse other projects or workspace billing. Invitations expire after seven days. If an invitation has expired, the owner must send a new invitation. Removing a guest immediately revokes their project access.

## Deleted project recovery

Deleted projects stay in the recycle bin for 30 days. An owner can restore a project during that period, including its tasks and documents. After 30 days, deletion is permanent. Deleting an entire workspace is different: it is permanent and cannot be reversed through the recycle bin.

## Exports and backups

Owners and editors can export a project as JSON, including tasks and document text. Viewers cannot export projects. Attachments are listed as download links rather than embedded file bytes. Download attachments separately before deleting a workspace. Exports are manual; the demo product does not provide scheduled backups.

## Notification preferences

Each member controls their own notification preferences. Instant email is the default. Daily digest sends one summary at 09:00 in the member's chosen time zone. Muting a project stops its activity notifications, but direct mentions still send an email. An owner cannot change another member's personal notification preferences.
